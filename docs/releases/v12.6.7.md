# Zotero TaskFlow 12.6.7

This patch removes the three quick-open buttons that repeated the same window modes already offered by the default-mode selector in Zotero preferences. The selector remains the single control for choosing the default workspace mode; the internal preference key and all three runtime opening modes are unchanged. Preferences are reorganized into workspace and appearance, task defaults and reminders, Zotero literature integration, and tag/data maintenance. Settings that had no runtime behavior (sound, Pomodoro durations, auto-archive, and academic-preset toggle) are removed from the interface; task time tracking and academic profiles remain available through their working controls.
